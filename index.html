Multi-Agent Research

DeepScope

GitHub →

The problem: Ask a chatbot a complex research question and it gives a shallow summary or makes things up — because it tries to process too much at once.

My solution: DeepScope works like a small research team. A lead coordinator breaks the question into focused tasks, assigns them to specialist AI agents who search the web and store findings in files (keeping their memory clean), then a fact-checker verifies everything before generating the final report.

Document Intelligence

NexusRAG

Live Demo ↗GitHub →

The problem: Upload a document and ask AI for a specific number (like a GPA or revenue) — it often gets it wrong because it only searches by meaning, not exact words.

My solution: NexusRAG uses a dual search strategy — searching by both the meaning of your question AND exact keywords. A secondary AI re-scores for relevance, and every answer includes the exact page number so you can verify it yourself.

E-commerce AI Analytics

Live Demo ↗GitHub →

The problem: Store owners need answers about their sales data but don't know SQL. When basic AI-to-SQL tools make a mistake, they just show an error with no recovery.

My solution: Ask any business question in plain English — the AI writes the query, runs it safely, and if a mistake occurs, it automatically figures out what went wrong and fixes the query itself. Then it creates clean charts and business tips.
